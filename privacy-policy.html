Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 4, 2026 · Last Updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Robertson Morales ("I", "me", or "my"), an individual operating the website robertsonmorales.com (the "Site") from the Philippines, collects, uses, and protects personal information from visitors ("you" or "your"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

I only collect personal information that you choose to provide directly, such as through a contact form or a direct email/message. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address

I do not collect payment information, as the Site does not process online payments. I do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (e.g. health, financial account numbers, government IDs) through the Site.

2. How I Use Your Information

Personal information you submit is used solely to respond to your inquiries and to provide the web development and design services you request. I do not send newsletters or marketing emails, and I do not use your contact information for any purpose beyond communicating with you about your inquiry or project.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Site uses the following third-party analytics tools to help me understand how visitors use the Site (e.g. pages viewed, traffic sources, and general device/browser information). These tools use cookies or similar tracking technologies:

  • Google Analytics — measures website traffic and usage patterns. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.
  • Metricool — measures website traffic and helps me understand engagement with the Site.

The Site does not display advertisements, does not use the Facebook Pixel, and does not use retargeting or behavioral advertising of any kind. No information collected through these analytics tools is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

4. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. The Site does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no uniform standard for how sites should interpret them.

5. How Your Information Is Shared

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. It is only shared with the third-party analytics providers named above, to the extent necessary for them to operate, and is never used for advertising purposes.

6. Data Retention & Security

I retain personal information you provide only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or complete the services requested, unless a longer retention period is required by law. I take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Philippine Residents (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

As the Site and its operator are based in the Philippines, personal information is also handled in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). You have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, to damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

To exercise any of the rights above, contact me using the details in the "Contact Me" section below.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If I become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Site, I will take steps to delete such information promptly.

9. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g. client project links, social media). I am not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites, and I encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in my practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date." I encourage you to review this policy periodically.
